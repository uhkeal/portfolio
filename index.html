About me

I’m an Informatics student at the University of Washington, focused on UX and product design. I like figuring out why something feels confusing and then finding a simpler way to communicate it.

My work has included a student finance app, a music discovery site, and an indoor navigation concept. I enjoy working in Figma and thinking through the structure and visual design of an experience, but I’m also interested in understanding how the things I design are actually built.

Outside of school, I’ve spent years making graphics, editing videos, and experimenting with digital design. I also care a lot about music, gaming, and fashion, which has shaped how I think about visual style and presentation.
